# Reviving a well-used MTB

![side_shot](/docs/assets/giaco/side_new.JPG)

## Introduction 

A good friend of mine asked me if I could do some maintenance on his MTB. A quite old Giacomelli 26 inch workhorse that has received a fair beating. I got the bike in a pretty deplorable condition: 

![side_shot_2](/docs/assets/giaco/side_old.jpg)

Nevertheless, the bike itself defintly had potential and had a lot of good quality components installed: Ritchey seatpost, stem and headset, Shimano Deore XT groupset (with integrated shifters in the brakes; I had never seen this on a MTB!).

## Diagnosis and work to do

Apart from first needing a thorough clean, there was quite a lot to do to get this bike in a safely rideable and also enjoyable state.

- Tires needed replacement
- Brakes did not work whatsover
- Shifting also was completely dead
- Saddle and grips were pretty destroyed
- Chain and cassette were rusted and worn
- Headset bearings were really crunchy
- Locking mechanism for the suspension was broken
